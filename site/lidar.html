LiDAR elevation-difference map

This map shows LiDAR-derived surface elevation differences across the Fylde corridor, comparing 2018 and 2022 government survey data. Blue indicates subsidence, red indicates uplift, and the mid-range indicates minimal change. This is a remotely sensed surface-elevation comparison, not a ground survey measurement, and should be read alongside the corresponding written reports.

Source: 2018–2022 LiDAR elevation differencing, Fylde coast corridor. Full-resolution source data available on request; see Reports for methodology.
